MarsTV App Store Distribution Exception, version 1.0
====================================================

As a special exception, the copyright holders of MarsTV grant permission to
convey an unmodified or modified object-code build of MarsTV through an
application distribution service that imposes technical protection measures
or terms of service which would otherwise be considered further restrictions
under section 10 of AGPL-3.0-or-later, provided that:

1. the complete corresponding source for that exact build is offered at no
   charge from a durable, publicly accessible URL shown in the application;
2. recipients retain every right granted by AGPL-3.0-or-later outside the
   distribution service;
3. the distributor does not add restrictions beyond those unavoidably imposed
   by the distribution service; and
4. all third-party licenses and attribution requirements continue to be met.

This exception does not grant rights to the MarsTV name, logo, signing
identities, store accounts, or other trademarks.

Only copyright holders may apply this exception to their contributions.
Contributors must sign the MarsTV Contributor License Agreement in CLA.md for
their contribution to be included in official store builds.
